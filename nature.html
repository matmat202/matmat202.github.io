Nature's intricacies

Eight small pieces of physics that do the opposite of what you would guess. A ball that comes back to you from under a desk. A pendulum that stands on its head. Three balls that collide infinitely often in finite time.

Each one is simulated from first principles, checked against exact limits or published experiments, and then animated from the numbers the simulation produced — nothing here is a drawing of what the physics is supposed to look like.
